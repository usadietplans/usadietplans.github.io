About

USA Diet Plans is an independent food information site. We publish original, practical guides and free in-browser tools to help readers cook better and eat better.

Our articles are written from scratch for this site. We do not copy content from other websites, and we aim to give advice that is accurate, realistic and useful for everyday cooking and eating.

The site is run by a small team of food enthusiasts. We are not doctors or dietitians, and nothing on this site is medical advice. If you have a health condition, talk to a qualified professional before changing your diet.
